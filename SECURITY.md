# Security policy

Jacobrakai Foundation operates public services and publishes security research. This policy covers both, and applies
to every repository in this organization.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Write to **security@jacobrakai.org**.

Include what you tested, the steps to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with it. A short description with a
reliable reproduction is more useful than a scanner report.

We acknowledge reports within three business days and give an assessment within ten. If a report takes longer to
assess, we say so rather than going quiet.

We do not pay for vulnerability reports. We are a small charity and our budget goes to the services themselves.

## Scope

In scope:

- `jacobrakai.org` and its subdomains
- `formapauperis.com` and `in.formapauperis.com`
- The published blocklist feed and its distribution
- Source code in repositories owned by this organization

Out of scope:

- **Decoy infrastructure.** We operate servers whose exposure is deliberate. Their open services, weak configurations
  and unusual responses are the point of the research, not defects. Findings about them are not vulnerabilities.
- Missing security headers, cookie flags or TLS configuration preferences with no demonstrated exploitability.
- Denial of service, volumetric testing, and load generated by automated scanning.
- Social engineering of staff, volunteers or contributors, and physical access.
- Reports generated by automated tooling without a demonstrated impact.

## Limits on testing

**Do not access, modify or delete data belonging to other people.** Forma Pauperis is used by people in active legal
proceedings. If you encounter anything that looks like another person's filing, stop, do not retain it, and tell us
what you saw in general terms. We would rather receive an incomplete report than have someone's court documents
copied in the course of confirming a finding.

Do not degrade availability for people using the services.

## Coordinated disclosure

We will agree a timeline with you. Absent agreement, we expect to disclose publicly ninety days after the report or
when a fix ships, whichever comes first. We will credit you by the name you choose, or not at all if you prefer.

## Safe harbour

If you make a good-faith effort to follow this policy, we will treat your research as authorised. We will not pursue
legal action against you and will not support action by others arising from your report. We will say so publicly if
asked.

This statement binds the Foundation only. It cannot authorise access to systems operated by anyone else, including
third-party services we depend on, and it does not displace any law that applies to you.

## If your address appears in our blocklist

That is not a security report and it reaches us faster at **feed@jacobrakai.org**. Include the address, the period in
question, and what you believe was misattributed. We publish how addresses are selected, and we correct entries that
are wrong.
