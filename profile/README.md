## Court forms

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jacobrakaiFoundation/.github/main/profile/diagram-forms-light.svg#gh-light-mode-only" alt="" width="960" /><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jacobrakaiFoundation/.github/main/profile/diagram-forms-dark.svg#gh-dark-mode-only" alt="" width="960" />

<small>Find a form · Answer the questions · Download a completed PDF</small>

[Forma Pauperis](https://formapauperis.com/) helps people find New Jersey court forms and work through them without an account.

<!-- form-counts:start -->
- **1,912 forms** — 712 official New Jersey Judiciary forms, plus about 1,200 county, municipal, federal and state-agency forms.
- **485 fill out online** — answer the questions in your browser and download a completed PDF.
<!-- form-counts:end -->

Other forms provide the blank official PDF and a typed answer sheet to copy by hand. Free, with no account. Answers are erased after 30 minutes of inactivity. The service prepares documents; it does not file them for you.

> Legal information, not legal advice.

Start somewhere specific:

- [**Fee waiver, form CN 11208**](https://in.formapauperis.com/nj/start/NJForms/f11208_filingfeewai/) — ask the court to waive its filing fee under Rule 1:13-2.
- [**Public records**](https://formapauperis.com/records/) — where to file an OPRA request and search deeds, in all 21 counties.
- [**Case guides**](https://formapauperis.com/records/life-of-an-action/) — sixteen step-by-step walkthroughs, with the rule behind each step.
- [**Lawyer complaints**](https://formapauperis.com/records/bar-complaints/) — ethics grievances and fee disputes: where they go in your county, and the deadlines.

## Security research and threat data

Research and data for people who operate online services. The Foundation runs internet-exposed decoy servers — SSH, FTP, telnet, MySQL, VNC, and email — and publishes what attacks them.

### Threat research

<small>Observe · Investigate · Publish</small>

[Investigations](https://jacobrakai.org/research/) of attacks observed by the decoy servers, each published with its evidence and with the limits of what that evidence supports. Static analysis only — nothing was executed. Attacker-supplied URLs are never fetched from a sensor, and no attribution is claimed beyond infrastructure.

[Read the research →](https://jacobrakai.org/research/)

## Related work

[Sic Semper Errata](https://sicsempererrata.com/) builds public-records software for New Jersey municipal clerks: receive a request, collect records against it, record a release decision for each one, and produce a response against the state's seven-day clock.

What exists today is a public demo running on invented sample records — see [what is available and what is planned](https://sicsempererrata.com/editions/). The municipal edition is planned and not available for purchase. The [Bellwether demo](https://demo.sicsempererrata.com/requests/) gives each visitor a temporary workspace and runs a limited number at a time, so it sometimes reports that it is at capacity.
